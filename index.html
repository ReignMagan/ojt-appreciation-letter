To the wonderful people of the Planning and Development Office,

As my On-the-Job Training (OJT) journey comes to an end, I would like to express my heartfelt gratitude to everyone who became part of this experience. Although my time here was relatively short, the lessons, experiences, and memories I gained will certainly stay with me as I continue growing, both as a student and as an individual.

I would also like to take this opportunity to personally thank each of you.

To Ma'am Cristina,

Thank you for patiently teaching me various tasks and, more importantly, the proper way to approach and handle problems. I am truly amazed by how organized you are when assigning and explaining tasks, making everything easier to understand and accomplish. I am especially grateful that you entrusted me with the task of creating a website. As someone who genuinely enjoys development, having the opportunity to work on something aligned with my interests made my OJT experience even more meaningful and enjoyable. It allowed me to apply my existing knowledge while improving my skills through actual hands-on experience. One of the most valuable lessons I learned from you is how to communicate professionally, particularly when providing responses, conclusions, and realistic estimates to others. It may seem like a simple skill, but it is something I will definitely carry with me in my future career.

To Ma'am Kyleen,

Although our time together was quite short, I genuinely appreciated our little random conversations. Thank you for teaching me the proper way to handle printing tasks and for sharing your knowledge with me. Those small interactions may have seemed ordinary, but they made my OJT experience more comfortable and memorable.

To Sir Jayped,

Thank you for entrusting me with various Excel tasks and teaching me more efficient ways to accomplish them. Through your guidance, I became more familiar with Excel and discovered techniques that helped improve my productivity. Of course, I still have a long way to go before I can call myself an Excel expert, but at least I am no longer the same Excel user I was before. One spreadsheet at a time, I suppose!

To Ma'am Cristyflor,

Thank you for giving me opportunities to work on Excel-related tasks and for patiently answering the occasional questions I had. I appreciate your willingness to help whenever I needed clarification. Even through simple tasks and brief conversations, I was able to learn something valuable, and I am grateful for that.

To Sir Charlie,

Thank you for our random conversations about AI and development. Although they were mostly casual discussions, I genuinely enjoyed exchanging thoughts about technology and its possibilities. As someone interested in the IT field, those conversations were especially interesting to me. Who knows? Maybe one day, some of those random AI ideas will actually turn into something useful!

To Sir Moscoso and Sir Jarvis,

Although we did not have many opportunities to interact, I still want to express my appreciation to both of you. Sometimes, even being part of a welcoming workplace is enough to make someone's experience meaningful. Thank you for being part of my OJT journey, even if our interactions were limited.

To the entire Planning and Development Office family,

Thank you very much for welcoming me and allowing me to experience what it is like to work in a professional environment. My stay here was not just about completing the required OJT hours or accomplishing assigned tasks. It was also about discovering my strengths, recognizing my weaknesses, and learning how to become a better version of myself.

Looking back, I can confidently say that I am more capable and knowledgeable than the person I was a few months ago. I gained practical skills, improved my understanding of workplace responsibilities, and learned lessons that cannot always be taught inside a classroom.

Of course, I would also like to apologize for my occasional awkwardness, my sometimes questionable ability to read the room, and those moments when my initiative seemed to be running on low battery. HAHAHA! I know I still have plenty of room for improvement, especially when it comes to communication, social awareness, and taking initiative without always waiting for instructions.

But jokes aside, this experience helped me realize that professional growth is not only about technical knowledge or academic achievements. It is also about learning how to communicate, adapt, collaborate, and interact with different people. These are areas I genuinely want to continue improving.

I may not have been the most talkative or socially skilled trainee in the office, but I sincerely appreciated every lesson, every opportunity, every conversation, and every bit of patience you shared with me.

As I move forward, I will carry these experiences as reminders that growth does not happen overnight. Sometimes, it begins with a simple Excel task, a printing assignment, a random conversation, or even an awkward moment that makes you reflect on yourself afterward.

Thank you guys for being part of my journey and for helping me become a better version of myself, not just academically or professionally, but personally as well.

I came here as a student trying to complete my OJT requirements, and I am leaving with more knowledge, better skills, meaningful experiences, and a clearer understanding of the things I still need to improve.

Once again, thank you so much, Planning and Development Office!

With sincere gratitude and appreciation,

Your slightly awkward,

but genuinely grateful OJT trainee ♡
